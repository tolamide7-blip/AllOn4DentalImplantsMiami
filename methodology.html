Methodology — How We Researched All-on-4 Pricing in Miami

Last updated Sept 9, 2026 | Author: T. B., independent researcher | Contact: hello@AllOn4DentalImplantsMiami.com | Not medical advice

We researched publicly listed prices for full-arch fixed implant treatment (marketed as All-on-4, teeth in a day) in Miami-Dade between Jan 15 and Aug 28, 2026.

Sample

  • 42 price points from websites, Google Business profiles, and phone inquiries to front desk / treatment coordinators.
  • Zip coverage: 33131, 33139, 33134, 33156, 33180, 33178 — 6 to 8 quotes each.
  • We excluded quotes >$35k per arch as outliers and required that quotes mention number of implants and type of final prosthesis where possible.

What we asked

"What is the starting price for 1 arch All-on-4 fixed, including surgery, temporary, and final zirconia, without extractions/grafts? What is added for extractions, bone graft, IV sedation?"

Calculation

  • Base per zip = median of observed starting prices.
  • Low = 25th percentile, High = 75th percentile.
  • Add-ons: Extractions median +$800 (range $500-$1200), Bone graft +$1800 (range $1200-$3000), IV sedation +$1000 (range $800-$1200) when billed separately.
  • Total for full mouth (2 arches) = per-arch figure x 2 — clearly shown in calculator.

Verifiability

Anonymized source table with date, zip, channel (website/phone), and quote range is published in Pricing Analysis. Full notes retained offline and available for review on request for audit purposes, with provider names redacted to avoid advertising.

Limitations

Illustrative observations only, not guarantees. Prices change, inclusion criteria vary, some providers bundle sedation. This is pricing research, not clinical guidance. For treatment decisions, consult a licensed dentist or oral surgeon.
