Cost Components — What is Typically Included in a Full-Arch Quote

Disclaimer: This is pricing research, not clinical advice. Inclusion varies by provider. Always ask for itemized written treatment plan.

  • Consultation & Imaging: CBCT, photos, scans — sometimes free, sometimes $150-$350.
  • Surgery: 4 implants (or more), surgical guide, anesthesia (local vs IV sedation). IV sedation often billed separately.
  • Extractions: If needed, $75-$200 per tooth, often $500-$1200 total for full arch.
  • Bone graft / sinus lift: All-on-4 often avoids graft by tilting posterior implants, but not always. $1200-$3000 when needed.
  • Temporary prosthesis: Fixed acrylic same day (where protocol allows) — often included.
  • Final prosthesis: Zirconia vs acrylic. Zirconia more durable, often $2000-$5000 more than acrylic. Our base assumes zirconia where stated.
  • Follow-ups: Adjustments, soft relines during healing.
  • What is often NOT included: Sedation, grafting, extractions, final upgrades, warranties.
