How Fixed Full-Arch Treatment Works — Educational Summary

Fixed full-arch implant treatment (commonly marketed as “all-on-four” or “teeth in a day”) typically uses four implants per arch to support a screw-retained full bridge. Per Malo et al. (2003, 2005), two anterior implants are typically placed vertically, two posterior implants tilted 30-45° to increase spread and avoid structures like maxillary sinus. This can reduce need for extensive grafting in some patients, though not all. A provisional prosthesis (often PMMA) may be placed same day if insertion torque is sufficient (commonly ≥35 Ncm), with final zirconia or titanium-acrylic hybrid after 3-6 months. Costs vary by material, lab, sedation, extractions, grafting. This summary is educational, based on sources linked below, not medical advice.

Sources: ADA — Dental Implants • Malo P et al. 2003 • Malo 2005 • FDA Dental Implants
